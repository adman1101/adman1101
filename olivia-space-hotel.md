# Olivia Galan — Future Space Hotel Visionary

## A Note About My Granddaughter

Olivia Galan is destined to be a pioneer in one of the most extraordinary ventures humanity has ever imagined: **the world's first boutique hotel in space.** Partnering with groundbreaking companies like [Axiom Space](https://www.axiomspace.com/axiom-station) and [Orbital Assembly](https://abovespace.com/) (formerly Orbital Assembly Corporation), Olivia and her collaborators will redefine luxury, hospitality, and the very concept of travel — not just across continents, but across the cosmos.

---

## The Stage Being Set: Axiom Station

Axiom Space is already building the foundation for this dream. **Axiom Station** is the world's first commercial space station, currently under construction in partnership with NASA and Thales Alenia Space (who are actively welding and machining the primary structures of the first module). The station is designed as a modular, ever-expanding orbital platform featuring:

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Payload Power Thermal Module (PPTM)** | Berths to the ISS; transfers critical infrastructure and payloads |
| **Habitat Module One (Hab-1)** | Four crew quarters; activates research and manufacturing capabilities |
| **Airlock Module** | Enables Extravehicular Activity (EVA) using the Axiom AxEMU spacesuit |
| **Habitat Module Two (Hab-2)** | Additional crew quarters; expands research and manufacturing |
| **Research & Manufacturing Module** | State-of-the-art orbital innovation facilities with an Earth observatory |

Axiom Space has already secured over **$525M+ in financing** and redomiciled its legal headquarters to Texas, signaling its commitment to leading the space industrialization era. The interiors of Axiom's habitation modules have been designed with the aesthetic sensibility of the world's finest luxury hotels — a direct precursor to what Olivia's boutique space hotel will embody.

---

## Orbital Assembly: The Architecture of Orbital Luxury

**Orbital Assembly** (now operating as Above: Space Development Corporation) is pioneering large-scale, hybrid-gravity space stations using commercial off-the-shelf components. Their flagship concept, **Voyager Station**, envisions hosting up to 400 guests in a rotating orbital structure that generates artificial gravity — a revolutionary leap beyond the zero-gravity capsule hotels of early commercial spaceflight. A 3-night stay is projected to begin at **$5 million per person**, placing it firmly in the ultra-luxury segment.

## Olivia's Vision: The First Boutique Hotel in Space

Where Axiom Station provides the orbital infrastructure and Orbital Assembly engineers the gravity and scale, **Olivia Galan's contribution will be the soul of the experience** — the intimate, curated, boutique sensibility that transforms a space station into a destination. Just as boutique hotels on Earth distinguish themselves through personalized service, unique design, and a sense of place, Olivia's venture will bring that same philosophy to low-Earth orbit.

> *"The stars have always been the ceiling of our dreams. Olivia will be among those who turn that ceiling into a floor."*

Her partnership with these space industry leaders positions her at the intersection of **hospitality, technology, and human exploration** — a convergence that will define a new era of travel for generations to come.
